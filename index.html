A 2-hour, coffee-shop-friendly workshop
for managers ready to turn meetings into searchable intelligence.

Pandan Labs  ·  v1.0  ·  Riyadh  ·  May 2026
Who it's for

Managers — tech or non-tech — in 8–12 meetings a week. If you've ever lost track of a commitment made in a call two weeks ago, this workshop is for you. No prior coding experience required.

What you'll build

decisions — a CLI that processes meeting transcripts, extracts decisions and action items, maintains a SQLite ledger, and generates weekly briefings.

What to bring

Claude Pro · Python 3.11+ · a laptop that runs terminal commands

› decisions-workshop  ·  2 hours  ·  managers  ·  Riyadh 2026

You've already pasted a meeting transcript into Claude or ChatGPT and got a good summary. But the summary disappeared when you closed the tab. Next week's transcript didn't know about last week's decisions. There was no ledger, no query, no accountability trail.

This workshop builds the thing a chat box structurally cannot: a tool that accumulates. Every meeting you process adds to the ledger. Every commitment is searchable by owner, deadline, and topic — across weeks, across meetings, across stakeholders.

What Will You Make

A CLI that turns a folder of meeting transcripts into a searchable ledger — in two hours.

After the Workshop — Pick a Track

Take the decisions tool somewhere you'd actually use it. Each track is an evening or a weekend.

Track A

Scheduled Weekly Briefing

The tool runs every Monday at 8am and emails you a structured briefing — open items, owners, upcoming deadlines. Set it once, never do it manually again.

Open Track
Track B

Google Drive / Notion Sync

decisions add pulls transcripts directly from a connected Drive folder or Notion database. No manual export step.

Open Track
Track C

Slack Channel Watch

An agent monitors a Slack channel where your team drops meeting summaries. New message → automatic processing → ledger updated. No manual trigger.

Open Track
Track D

MCP + Claude Desktop

Ask Claude Desktop: "What's still open from the SAMA meeting?" — it queries your live ledger and answers with real data, not a guess.
