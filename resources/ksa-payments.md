# KSA Payment Notes

Anthropic officially supports Saudi Arabia for both Claude.ai and the API as of May 2026. Most Saudi cards work. This page is for when yours doesn't.

## Cards that work

- **Mada** (most common Saudi debit card) — works for Claude Pro subscriptions.
- **Visa / Mastercard** issued by Saudi banks — generally work.
- **AMEX** — works.

## If your card is declined

**Try a different card first.** Some banks' fraud systems flag international SaaS subscriptions and decline the first attempt. A second attempt with the same card sometimes succeeds.

**Contact your bank.** Ask them to whitelist international online subscriptions. Some Saudi banks require a one-time authorisation call before the first international transaction.

**Prepaid options:**
- Virtual Visa/Mastercard cards issued by STC Pay or Urpay work as an alternative.
- Apple Pay with a linked Saudi card works on Claude.ai.

**No VPN needed.** Anthropic directly serves Saudi Arabia — don't use a VPN to subscribe. It can trigger additional fraud checks.

## Corporate billing

If you're subscribing for a team through a company account, Claude Pro allows up to 5 seats. For larger teams, Anthropic offers enterprise contracts — contact their sales team directly.

## The workshop covers Pro, not Teams or Enterprise

Claude Pro ($20/month) is sufficient for the workshop. Teams or Enterprise plans have more seats and higher rate limits but are not required.
